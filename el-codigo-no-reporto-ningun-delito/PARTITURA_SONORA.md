# PARTITURA SONORA

## An intrepid rhythm section inside an unstable archive

**Status:** musical concept · version 0.1  
**Priority:** text first; sound carries pressure, state and consequence

---

## 1. Musical proposition

The score is driven by bass and drums: alert, physical, investigative, capable of accelerating and decelerating as though the case itself were changing metabolic rate.

Synthesizer and orchestral color do not provide cinematic grandeur. They reveal distance, institutional scale, missing information and the unstable aura surrounding the disputed work.

The music should feel intrepid rather than heroic.

> The rhythm section follows transactions.  
> The orchestra follows belief.  
> The synthesizer follows the file.

---

## 2. Hierarchy

1. Text and reading remain primary.
2. Bass and drums articulate investigative momentum.
3. Synthesizer exposes technical states and mutations.
4. Orchestral fragments widen the historical or emotional field.
5. Silence marks irrecoverable evidence and permits sustained reading.

Sound must never demand continuous attention or punish slow readers.

---

## 3. Ensemble

### Bass

Electric or synthetic bass with a dry, articulate attack. It carries routes, recurrences and economic pressure.

Possible behaviors:

- walking but asymmetrical investigation line;
- ostinato whose missing note represents unavailable evidence;
- register shifts when provenance changes jurisdiction;
- notes sold out of the pattern until only rhythm remains;
- return of a bass figure under a different harmonic ownership.

### Drums

Acoustic-informed kit or tightly designed samples. Avoid generic techno pulse.

Functions:

- hi-hat as query activity;
- kick as settled transaction;
- snare/rim as institutional assertion;
- toms as route changes;
- brushed or granular noise as archive handling;
- dropped beat as missing link.

### Synthesizer

Represents mutable media, cryptographic certainty and unstable display.

Use:

- narrow-band pulses for verified operations;
- detuned duplicates for competing file versions;
- wavetable or spectral drift for migration;
- stable pitch with changing timbre for stable token / unstable media;
- filtered noise for dead links and overwritten metadata.

### Orchestra

Small, fragmentary and sometimes synthetic: low strings, divided violas, bass clarinet, muted brass, occasional high harmonic or prepared-piano color.

It should arrive as historical pressure, not as soundtrack climax.

Possible gestures:

- a chord whose missing inner voice is the absent author;
- strings sustaining the memory of text already removed;
- brass entering only when an institution canonizes a claim;
- orchestral tuning fragments around the photograph;
- a single warm sonority for the studio memories.

---

## 4. Tempo as epistemic state

Tempo is not a progress meter. It reflects the rate at which claims are being connected.

### 54–68 BPM — inspection

Sparse bass, room tone, space for long paragraphs.

### 82–104 BPM — pursuit

Evidence links become legible; rhythm gains direction.

### 118–148 BPM — proliferation

Buttons multiply, citations refer to citations, the investigator acts faster than they can verify.

### Deceleration without resolution

The pattern loses events while the nominal tempo remains or bends downward. This accompanies sold, burned or orphaned evidence.

### False return

The opening tempo returns after the loop, but accents have shifted and a beat previously felt as “one” is now heard as an anacrusis. The reader returns to the same page from a different grammatical ownership.

Transitions should be eased over time, not switched abruptly unless an evidence theft requires a cut.

---

## 5. Metric instability

The rhythm should be adventurous but readable.

- begin with clear four-beat grounding;
- insert bars of 3, 5 or shortened cycles when evidence disappears;
- use additive groupings such as 3+3+2 or 4+4+3 for institutional complexity;
- allow bass and drums to disagree temporarily about phrase length;
- restore alignment when a claim is canonized, even if that claim is false.

The effect is a mind reorganizing incomplete information, not virtuosity for its own sake.

---

## 6. Evidence sonification

| Evidence event | Musical consequence |
|---|---|
| signature verifies | dry pulse locks to grid |
| signer disappears | bass loses its lowest note |
| photograph enlarges | orchestral harmonics narrow into noise |
| page is torn | one beat is permanently omitted |
| file is substituted | pitch sequence remains; timbre changes |
| price rises | bass register rises while harmony cools |
| testimony is purchased | voice-like synth loses consonant attack |
| evidence is laundered | motif returns with cleaner orchestration and altered source |
| document is burned | sound leaves a filtered resonance |
| route becomes evidence | visitor's navigation rhythm becomes the next loop's ostinato |

These mappings should be perceptible across repetition, not explained during play.

---

## 7. Reading-aware behavior

The score does not assume a fixed duration.

Each chamber contains layered musical states that can sustain without obvious looping:

- entry gesture;
- reading bed;
- interaction response;
- departure mutation;
- optional silence.

Long text lowers event density. Hover and focus may reveal small timbral relations, but sound must not require frantic interaction.

If the page loses focus, the work should thin or pause gracefully. It should never continue an aggressive rhythm invisibly.

---

## 8. The role of silence

Silence is an authored state.

Use it when:

- an irreplaceable passage is surrendered;
- a witness cannot be found;
- the work appears only as dimensions;
- the visitor realizes their route has been cataloged;
- a chamber contains a long intimate memory;
- the loop is about to restart.

Some evidence should remove sound rather than add it. A completely investigated session may become musically poorer.

---

## 9. Voice and speech

Avoid continuous narration. The prose is for reading.

Possible vocal material:

- isolated bureaucratic labels;
- a breath or clipped syllable from the voice note;
- numbers spoken without explanatory nouns;
- several speakers repeating a sentence with changed authority;
- inaccessible text leaving a phonetic residue.

Any speech must remain optional and subtitled. Never make audio the sole carrier of evidence.

---

## 10. Browser-native implementation principles

The eventual score can be generated with Web Audio and authored sample material, without a server.

The hash determines:

- base pulse family;
- bass contour;
- timbral palette;
- orchestral absence/presence;
- metric fault;
- evidence-to-motif assignments.

The visitor determines:

- density through navigation;
- tempo pressure through accumulation;
- missing notes through sacrifice;
- orchestration through accepted hypotheses;
- the ostinato inherited by the loop.

Sound state must be reproducible from edition seed plus route when practical.

---

## 11. References by quality, not imitation

Useful musical qualities to discuss later:

- rhythm sections with investigative forward motion;
- post-punk or jazz-informed bass independence;
- progressive metric flexibility without bombast;
- electroacoustic timbral mutation;
- orchestral modernism used sparingly;
- library-music precision and institutional unease;
- groove that can fracture and recover;
- dynamic restraint under dense reading.

We should later choose concrete references for production, but the score must develop its own grammar from the evidence system.

---

## 12. Anti-patterns

- generic cyberpunk arpeggios;
- constant ominous drones;
- trailer-like orchestral swells;
- music that grows merely because the page is “getting intense”;
- sonification of every mouse movement;
- rhythmic density under every dense paragraph;
- a triumphant solution cue;
- inaccessible evidence conveyed only in sound;
- randomized notes without formal memory.

---

## 13. Final musical arc

The first encounter begins with an incomplete but confident bass figure.

As evidence accumulates, drums become more capable and less trustworthy. The synthesizer preserves pitch while changing identity. The orchestra arrives when claims acquire history. At maximum proliferation, the rhythm section moves with exhilarating competence while the reader can no longer verify why.

Then evidence is sold, burned and absorbed. The ensemble loses material. At the administrative conclusion, only the route rhythm remains.

The loop restarts. The opening bass figure returns, now built from the visitor's own decisions.

> The case did not acquire a solution.  
> It acquired a groove.
