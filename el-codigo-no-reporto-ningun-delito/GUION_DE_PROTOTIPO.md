# PROTOTYPE SCRIPT

## Sixteen chambers in which a reader becomes the provenance of an investigation

**Status:** complete conceptual walkthrough · version 0.1  
**Scope:** minimum viable literary experience, not production code  
**Estimated duration:** 25–40 minutes

---

## 1. Purpose

The prototype must demonstrate the entire conceptual transformation before the work expands to 256 chambers.

It must prove that:

- the case is compelling as literature;
- technical distinctions can be understood through reading;
- paying for information materially changes language;
- a button can steal grammatical agency;
- market activity can produce documentary certainty;
- the investigator can emerge as a second disputed object;
- the visitor's actions can become a misleading first-person statement;
- the final report can convert a route into provenance;
- bass, drums, synth and orchestral fragments can express state without overwhelming prose;
- the loop changes the status of the opening.

If these sixteen chambers do not work, adding the other 240 will only enlarge the failure.

# CHAMBER 1

## 184 — Appointment

**Coordinate:** T2 / Investigator-route / Observe  
**Dramatic position:** outside the record  
**Corpus:** C001, C002, C003

### Screen

A calm editorial field. The Continuity Office commission occupies the reading column. The 16 × 16 grid is faint and largely unlabeled. No portfolio is visible.

Opening lines:

> The Continuity Office requests an independent review of the provenance associated with *A Form of Remaining Outside*.

> The Office welcomes every possible conclusion compatible with the continued responsible stewardship of the work.

### Actions

Primary:

> ACCEPT APPOINTMENT

Secondary:

> INSPECT CURRENT CUSTODIAN

Refusal:

> DECLINE APPOINTMENT

### Consequences

#### Accept

- role becomes `assistant`;
- credibility +1;
- liability remains 0;
- investigator notes become directly addressed to “you.”

#### Inspect custodian

- opens a short relation showing that the Continuity Office owns the work;
- context +1;
- appointment letter gains a visible conflict-of-interest mark;
- acceptance remains available.

#### Decline

- role becomes `independent visitor`;
- credibility −1;
- liability remains 0;
- public-access route continues;
- later institutional report cites the refusal as evidence of openness.

### Sound

- incomplete bass gesture, two notes and a withheld third;
- low room tone;
- no drums;
- sound remains optional and starts only after visitor consent.

### Exit condition

Any choice leads to Chamber 065. The role difference remains active.

# CHAMBER 2

## 065 — The valid signature

**Coordinate:** T1 / Signature / Verify  
**Dramatic position:** first narrow certainty  
**Corpus:** C010, C011, C012, C014

### Screen

The signed message appears in a compact technical field:

```text
recognized: AF-0-17
for continuity
not the blue one
```

Below it:

> SIGNATURE VALID

The sentence is initially reassuring.

### Reveal

The verification scope expands:

> It establishes that the message was signed using a private key corresponding to the historical address.

Then exclusions appear one at a time:

> It does not establish who possessed the key.  
> It does not establish what the identifier pointed to.  
> It does not establish whether the signer considered that identifier a work.

### Action

> ASSIGN WORKING LABEL

Choices:

- `ARTIST`;
- `STUDIO`;
- `UNKNOWN CUSTODIAN`;
- `SHARED KEY`.

The reader may also:

> CONTINUE WITHOUT HUMAN LABEL

### Consequences

- assigning a label gains operational clarity and 1 credibility;
- `ARTIST` adds 1 liability because the label exceeds the proof;
- continuing without label costs 1 context later when institutional documents require a named actor;
- selected label becomes portable and can appear in later prose.

### Sound

- signature verification locks one synth pulse to the grid;
- first kick-drum event;
- bass withholds its lowest note when human identity remains unresolved.

### Visual residue

```text
SIGNATURE VALID / SIGNER [SELECTED LABEL]
```

or:

```text
SIGNATURE VALID / SIGNER UNASSIGNED
```

## 180 — Testimony: retain, sell or return

**Coordinate:** T2 / Artist-witness / Acquire  
**Dramatic position:** first serious ethical cost  
**Corpus:** C022, C073, L02

### Screen

The intact testimony reappears beside three possibilities.

### Option A — Retain

> KEEP IN CUSTODY

- occupies one slot;
- preserves full language;
- closes immediate access to one sale document unless another evidence is displaced;
- liability unchanged.

### Option B — Sell

> SELL TESTIMONY

- receive 9 context + 1 free custody;
- full language disappears from future chambers;
- institutional summary remains;
- liability +2 because the text was received as a gift;
- Market gains its sensory details as tradeable descriptors.

### Option C — Return

> RETURN TO WITNESS

- testimony leaves portfolio;
- no resources received;
- it cannot be quoted in the final report;
- one resistant route opens later;
- the witness's long sentence remains readable here;
- credibility −1, liability unchanged.

### Summary after sale

> Former collaborator confirms blue-screen behavior in the artist's studio.

The loss must feel unmistakable.

### Sound

- retain: warm orchestral fragment remains private to quiet chambers;
- sell: consonant detail disappears; motif becomes clean market signal;
- return: musical fragment ends without cadence and does not enter portfolio music.

## 173 — Information run

**Coordinate:** T2 / Sale / Cut-transfer  
**Dramatic position:** exhilarating competence and semantic panic  
**Corpus:** interface verbs, residues, C042, C082

### Trigger

The Market announces:

> CONFIDENCE REMAINS HIGH.  
> ACCESS TO SUPPORTING CONTEXT MAY BE INTERRUPTED.

### Proliferation tree

```text
LOCATE OWNER
├─ LOCATE ADDRESS
│  ├─ LOCATE REGISTERED AGENT
│  │  ├─ PURCHASE JURISDICTION
│  │  └─ VERIFY DISSOLUTION
│  └─ INSPECT CURRENT OCCUPANT
└─ LOCATE AUTHORITY TO LOCATE
```

Additional offers:

> PRESERVE QUOTATION  
> SELL BEFORE CORRECTION  
> ABANDON SOURCE  
> CONVERT MEMORY  
> EXIT WITH ATTRIBUTION

### Word theft

Source sentence:

> The Archive preserved the uncertainty by assigning it a permanent identifier.

The button extracts `preserved`.

Result:

> The Archive ________ the uncertainty by assigning it a permanent identifier.

Acquired action:

> PRESERVE CURRENT ATTRIBUTION

### Time

There is no countdown. The visitor can stop and read. Pressure comes from multiplication, rhythm and the increasing conversion of paragraphs into claims.

### Exit conditions

- take any two market actions;
- or choose `STOP TRADING` after inspecting the consequences;
- or allow liability saturation to end the run.

Every route preserves the stolen verb as mobile evidence.

### Sound

- tempo rises through smooth stages;
- bass and drums temporarily disagree on phrase length;
- synth pulses multiply with offers;
- action sounds remain distinguishable;
- reduced-motion/audio mode shows the same ancestry as a static tree.

# CHAMBER 14

## 188 — The visitor's statement

**Coordinate:** T2 / Investigator-route / Acquire  
**Dramatic position:** procedural traces become first person  
**Corpus:** C112

### Screen

Actions appear beside generated language:

```text
VERIFY SIGNATURE       → I verified the artist's signature,
PURCHASE DATE          → dated the earliest image,
ESTABLISH/ACCEPT       → established the original account,
REMOVE/ABANDON         → and excluded conflicting material.
```

The exact sentence varies according to route.

### Rules

- it must be locally accurate;
- it must conceal costs through fluent syntax;
- it must not invent an action the visitor did not take;
- refusal must also be convertible into institutional language;
- sold or missing qualifiers alter certainty.

### Choices

> SIGN STATEMENT  
> CORRECT STATEMENT  
> ATTRIBUTE TO INVESTIGATOR  
> CONTINUE WITHOUT AUTHOR

#### Sign

- liability +2;
- visitor becomes named procedural author within the route, not real-world identified.

#### Correct

- spends remaining context;
- restores one qualification if available;
- report becomes longer and less conclusive.

#### Attribute

- investigator acquires the visitor's actions;
- visitor retains apparent independence;
- investigator personhood strengthens.

#### Continue without author

- passive voice replaces the first person;
- Continuity Office supplies responsible authority later.

### Visual transformation

Arrows and source operations gradually fade. The paragraph remains.

### Sound

- each action contributes a rhythmic unit;
- the assembled sentence produces the route ostinato;
- grammatical smoothing aligns the pattern to a stable meter.

# ROUTE VARIANTS

## 4. Witness-preserving route

The visitor retains the full testimony.

Effects:

- less context available for sale analysis;
- final report admits economic uncertainty;
- witness language remains intact;
- route receives lower institutional confidence;
- second loop contains a longer resistant passage;
- orchestral warmth survives without becoming a market motif.

This is not declared the ethical winner. Retention is still custody.

---

## 5. Liquid route

The visitor sells testimony and purchases sale context.

Effects:

- circular relation becomes clearer;
- report is technically richer;
- intimate language becomes institutional summary;
- liability increases;
- Market values route highly;
- next loop cites visitor as independent financial researcher.

---

## 6. Refusal route

The visitor declines appointment, precise dating and investigator classification.

Effects:

- less authority;
- more intact uncertainty;
- documents appear through public-access frames;
- final report may be “unfiled inquiry”;
- Continuity Office cites refusal as openness;
- reader still becomes evidence of dissent accommodation.

---

## 7. Canonical route

The visitor assigns artist, buys 1998, accepts Orr, sells testimony and signs the statement.

Effects:

- cleanest report;
- highest institutional confidence;
- fewest visible contradictions;
- greatest liability;
- route is cataloged as exemplary independent confirmation;
- second loop begins with difficult-to-access dissent.

## 16. Acceptance test session

A prototype session succeeds when a reader can answer:

- What exactly did the signature prove?
- Why did purchasing the date weaken the photograph?
- How did the first collector acquire apparent personhood?
- What did selling or retaining testimony change?
- Where did the stolen verb go?
- How did the visitor's actions become a first-person claim?
- Why does the final report sound more certain than the evidence?
- In what sense did the visitor become provenance?
- Why can the case continue without a definitive culprit?

The reader need not remember interface-resource numbers to answer these.

---

## 17. Failure indicators

Revise the prototype if:

- readers perceive a puzzle with a hidden correct solution;
- prices feel like game points;
- the witness passage feels expendable before the offer appears;
- proliferation reads as random visual noise;
- readers miss the difference between signature and identity;
- the investigator twist feels unrelated to the original case;
- the interface blames readers for consequences it concealed;
- music makes long passages harder to read;
- the report does not reflect actual choices;
- the loop feels like a restart rather than reclassification;
- the visual field looks like generic crypto or archival styling;
- readers remember mechanics but not sentences.
